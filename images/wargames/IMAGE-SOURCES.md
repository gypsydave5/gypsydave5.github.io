## The Contrast You Want

MONSTER (wrong abstraction): Photo of War in Europe or Drang Nach Osten mid-game with dense counter stacks filling hexes. Unreadable to anyone who hasn't been staring at it.

STRATEGIC (right abstraction): Photo of Paths of Glory or Twilight Struggle mid-game. You can SEE what's happening — fronts, pressure, momentum — at a glance.

The argument: Code is the monster wargame. When AI is generating features, you need the strategic game view.
